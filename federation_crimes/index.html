The Russian Federation retains the Soviet Union’s imperial ambitions. It continues its atrocious conquest towards Ossetians, Chechens, Georgians, and Ukrainians, committing mass murders, executions, torture targeting prisoners or war and civillians.

South Ossetia war (1991–1992) 05.01.1991

The 1991-1992 South Ossetia War, involving Russian-backed Ossetian separatists, resulted in around 1,000 deaths and displaced over 63,000 people.

Battle of Grozny (1994–1995) 31.12.1994

The Russian invasion of Grozny, from December 1994 to March 1995, involved heavy artillery and air strikes, leading to significant civilian casualties, estimated between 5,000 to 30,000 deaths.

Samashki massacre 07.04.1995

Russian forces killed over 100 civilians in Samashki in April 1995, shooting and burning them alive, and destroying the village, including the school.

Second Chechen War 26.08.1999

The Second Chechen War (1999-2009) saw Russian forces deploy banned weapons and indiscriminately target civilians, leading to up to 25,000 civilian deaths and 5,000 forced disappearances.

1999 Russian apartment bombings 04.09.1999

The 1999 Russian apartment bombings killed over 300 and injured over 1,000, instigating fear to justify upcoming the Second Chechen War for civillians. Independent investigations suggest Russian state security orchestrated the attacks to escalate conflict and boost Putin's popularity.

Battle of Grozny (1999–2000) 25.12.1999

The Battle of Grozny (1999-2000) involved extensive Russian bombardment, leading to Grozny's designation as the most destroyed city on Earth. Between 5,000 and 8,000 civilians were killed, and severe human rights violations occurred.

Bombing of Katyr-Yurt 04.02.2000

Russian forces bombed Katyr-Yurt and a refugee convoy on February 4, 2000, killing at least 363 civilians.

Novye Aldi massacre 05.02.2000

Russian forces executed up to 82 civilians in Novye Aldi during a cleansing operation on February 5, 2000, accompanied by looting, rape, and arson.

Komsomolskoye massacre 03.2000

During the Komsomolskoye massacre, about 72 surrendered Chechen combatants were almost entirely killed or disappeared after detention and torture.

Moscow theater hostage crisis 23.10.2002

The Moscow theater hostage crisis, involving 912 hostages taken by 40 Chechen terrorists, ended with 132 hostage deaths after Russian forces used an undisclosed chemical agent.

Beslan school siege 01.09.2004

Beslan school siege, lasting 3 days in 2004, involved over 1,100 hostages, including 777 children, leading to 334 deaths, 186 of them children.

Russo-Georgian War 07.08.2008

The 2008 Russo-Georgian War, initiated by Russia, resulted in the displacement of 192,000 people and widespread civilian casualties.

Russo-Ukrainian war 20.02.2014

The Russian invasion of Ukraine began in February 2014 and is ongoing. Estimated casualties are between 1,274,000 and 2,000,000, of which a minimum of 20,000 civilians killed and 50,000 wounded.

Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 17.07.2014

Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 was shot down by a Buk missile from a launcher associated with Russian-backed separatists in eastern Ukraine, killing all 298 passengers and crew.

Battle of Debaltseve 14.01.2015

The Battle of Debaltseve, part of the war in Donbas, resulted in over 267 Ukrainian deaths and severe humanitarian suffering, with nearly 500 civilian corpses found post-battle.

Siege of Mariupol 24.02.2022

The Russian siege of Mariupol lasted nearly 3 months, resulting in the city's occupation and the death of at least 1,348 civilians, with the true toll estimated to be thousands higher. The siege also caused the destruction of 90% of the city's residential buildings, while Russian forces blocked humanitarian aid, committing numerous war crimes.

Bombing of Borodianka 01.03.2022

On 1–2 March 2022, Russian airstrikes destroyed 8 multi-storey residential buildings in Borodianka, killing 40-80 civilians.

Kharkiv government building airstrike 01.03.2022

On 1 March 2022, two Russian cruise missiles struck the Kharkiv Regional State Administration building and nearby Freedom Square, killing 29 people and injuring more than 35.

3 March 2022 Chernihiv bombing 03.03.2022

On 3 March 2022, Russian aircraft bombed a residential area in Chernihiv, killing 47 and injuring 18, while civilians were going about their daily activities.

Irpin refugee column shelling 06.03.2022

On 6 March 2022, Russian forces repeatedly shelled an intersection in Irpin being used by civilians fleeing toward Kyiv, killing 8.

9 March 2022 Mariupol Hospital 3 attack 09.03.2022

On 9 March 2022, Russian forces bombed Maternity Hospital No. 3 in Mariupol, killing at least 4 people and injuring at least 16.

Bucha massacre 11.03.2022

The Bucha massacre involved the mass murder of Ukrainian civilians by Russian forces, with 458 bodies recovered, including 9 children. The UN documented at least 73 unlawful killings, and Human Rights Watch confirmed war crimes by Russian troops, including executions and torture.

Stara Krasnianka care house attack 11.03.2022

On 11 March 2022, Russia-affiliated forces shelled a care home at Stara Krasnianka with heavy weapons while 71 disabled patients and 15 staff remained inside. A fire followed and 56 civilians died.

16 March 2022 Chernihiv breadline attack 16.03.2022

On 16 March 2022, Russian forces attacked civilians queuing for bread in Chernihiv, killing 18 and injuring 26.

Mariupol theatre airstrike 16.03.2022

The Russian Armed Forces bombed the Mariupol theatre on March 16, 2022, used as a civilian air raid shelter, causing up to 600 deaths.

Mykolaiv government building missile strike 29.03.2022

On 29 March 2022, Russian forces launched a cruise missile at the Mykolaiv Regional State Administration building, destroying roughly half of the structure and triggering fires. The strike killed 37 people and injured 34.

Kramatorsk railway station attack 08.04.2022

On April 8, 2022, a Russian missile strike hit Kramatorsk's railway station, killing 63 civilians, including 9 children, and wounding 150.

Bilohorivka school bombing 07.05.2022

On 7 May 2022, Russian forces bombed a school in Bilohorivka where about 90 people were sheltering in the basement. The strike caused a fire and trapped people inside. Up to 60 civilians died.

Kremenchuk shopping mall attack 27.06.2022

On June 27, 2022, Russian missiles hit the Kremenchuk shopping mall, killing at least 20 people and injuring 59.

Serhiivka missile strike 01.07.2022

The Russian missile strike in Serhiivka on July 1, 2022, killed at least 21 civilians and injured 38, including 6 children.

July 2022 Chasiv Yar missile strike 09.07.2022

On 9 July 2022, Russian forces fired Uragan missiles at residential apartment buildings in Chasiv Yar, killing at least 48 people.

Vinnytsia strikes (2022–present) 14.07.2022

On July 14, 2022, Russian naval forces launched Kalibr missiles at central Vinnytsia, striking a medical center and the House of Officers, killing 28+ and injuring 202+ civilians.

Olenivka prison massacre 29.07.2022

Russian forces shelled a Ukrainian prison in Olenivka, killing 53 Ukrainian prisoners of war and wounding 75-130, with independent investigations pointing to Russia's responsibility and Russian refusal to cooperate with UN investigations.

Kharkiv dormitories missile strike 17.08.2022

On 17–18 August 2022, Russian forces struck residential dormitories in Kharkiv with missiles, killing 25 people, and injuring 44.

Chaplyne railway station attack 24.08.2022

On 24 August 2022, Russian forces carried out a missile strike on Chaplyne, killing 25 people and injuring 31.

Izium mass graves 15.09.2022

After Ukrainian forces recaptured Izium during the Kharkiv counteroffensive, 447 bodies were discovered in mass graves, including 414 civilians (215 women, 194 men, 5 children) and 22 servicemen.

Kupiansk civilian convoy shelling 25.09.2022

On 25 September 2022, Russian soldiers attacked a civilian evacuation convoy near Kupiansk, killing 26 and allowing 22 to escape while they were fleeing the city.

Zaporizhzhia civilian convoy attack 30.09.2022

On 30 September 2022, Russian forces launched missiles at a civilian convoy near Zaporizhzhia as people were gathering to travel into Russian-occupied territory, killing 32 and injuring approximately 88.

2022 Zaporizhzhia residential building airstrike 09.10.2022

On 9 October 2022, Russian forces launched missiles at a residential area in Zaporizhzhia, destroying an apartment building, killing 13 and injuring 89.

2023 Dnipro residential building airstrike 14.01.2023

On 14 January 2023, Russian forces struck a 9-storey apartment building in Dnipro with a Kh-22 missile, killing 46 and injuring 80 civilians.

April 2023 Sloviansk airstrike 14.04.2023

On 14 April 2023, Russian missiles struck residential buildings in Sloviansk during Orthodox Good Friday, killing 15 civilians, including a child, and injuring 24.

2023 Uman missile strike 28.04.2023

On 28 April 2023, Russian forces struck a residential apartment building in Uman with a Kh-101 cruise missile, killing 23 civilians and injuring 9.

Destruction of the Kakhovka Dam 06.06.2023

Russian forces breached the Kakhovka Dam on June 6, 2023, causing catastrophic flooding downstream. At least 59 were officially reported dead, with potential casualties reaching into the hundreds.

2023 Kramatorsk restaurant missile strike 27.06.2023

On 27 June 2023, Russian forces launched 2 missiles at Kramatorsk, hitting a crowded civilian restaurant and killing 13, including 4 children, while 65 were injured.

Lyman cluster bombing 08.07.2023

On 8 July 2023, Russian forces used Smerch cluster munitions against a residential area in central Lyman, killing 9 and injuring 13 civilians.

2023 Pokrovsk missile strike 07.08.2023

On 7 August 2023, Russian forces struck Pokrovsk twice with Iskander missiles, killing 10 and injuring 88, including emergency personnel who had arrived at the first impact site.

August 2023 Chernihiv missile strike 19.08.2023

On August 19, 2023, a Russian Iskander-M missile struck central Chernihiv near Taras Shevchenko Theatre, killing 7 and injuring 144+, while an exhibition involving civilians was taking place.

Hroza missile attack 05.10.2023

On October 5, 2023, a Russian Iskander missile struck a café in Hroza during a memorial gathering for a Ukrainian soldier, killing 59 civilians and injuring 7.

Volnovakha massacre 27.10.2023

On 27 October 2023, two Russian soldiers allegedly entered a civilian family's home in occupied Volnovakha and killed all 9 members of the Kapkanets family, including children aged 5 and 9. The victims were reportedly shot after refusing to surrender their home for use by a Russian military unit.

2024 Pokrovsk missile strike 06.01.2024

On 6 January 2024, Russian forces launched a missile strike on a residential area in Pokrovsk, killing 12 people, including 6 children, and injuring 10.

15 March 2024 Odesa missile strikes 15.03.2024

On March 15, 2024, Russian forces struck Odesa, killing 21 and injuring 74.

25 May 2024 Kharkiv missile strikes 25.05.2024

On 25 May 2024, Russian forces struck Kharkiv, including the Epicentr K hypermarket, killing 19 and injuring 65+.

8 July 2024 Russian strikes on Ukraine 08.07.2024

On 8 July 2024, Russian strikes across Ukraine killed 47 and injured about 170, with a missile hitting the Okhmatdyt children's hospital in Kyiv.

September 2024 Poltava strike 03.09.2024

On September 3, 2024, Russian missiles hit a military institute and hospital in Poltava, Ukraine, killing at least 59 and injuring 328.

2025 Sumy airstrike 13.04.2025

On 13 April 2025, Russian ballistic missiles struck central Sumy during religious services, killing 35, including 2 children, and injuring 129, with 15 of the injured being children.

2025 Yarova attack 09.09.2025

On 9 September 2025, Russian forces struck Yarova with a glide bomb while pensioners were gathering to receive their pensions, killing 25 and injuring 19.

2025 Ternopil strike 19.11.2025

On 19 November 2025, Russian forces carried out a large combined missile and drone attack on Ternopil, striking residential areas and two apartment buildings. The attack killed at least 38 civilians, including 8 children, and injured 99 people.
