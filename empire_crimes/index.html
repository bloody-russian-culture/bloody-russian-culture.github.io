Bloody Russian Empire

The Russian Empire was built by conquest and kept by terror. It took Siberia from indigenous people, cleared the North Caucasus of the Circassians, executed and exiled Poles, and organised pogroms against Ukrainians, Jews, and Roman Catholics.

Russian conquest of Siberia 1581

The Russian conquest of Siberia involved significant violence and coercion against indigenous populations, with over 80% of the Tungus and Yakut populations dying from smallpox brought by Russian explorers between 1650s and 1690s.

Koliivshchyna 1768

The Koliivshchyna rebellion in Right-bank Ukraine in 1768, fueled by Russian support, led to the massacre of an estimated 100,000 to 200,000 victims, including Poles, Jews, and Roman Catholics.

Caucasian War 1817

The Caucasian War, spanning the 19th century, involved the Russian Empire's conquest of the North Caucasus, causing over 700,000 civilian deaths.

January Uprising 22.01.1863

The January Uprising against Russian imperial rule in Poland resulted in severe reprisals, including the execution of 396 persons and the exile of 18,672 to Siberia. Over 60,000 people were imprisoned and exiled.

Circassian genocide 21.05.1864

The Russian Empire carried out systematic mass killings and ethnic cleansing, and forcibly displaced between 95% and 97% of the Circassian people, resulting in up to 1,500,000 deaths.

Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) 24.04.1877

The Russo-Turkish War (1877-1878) was marked by significant civilian casualties, including the massacre of 14,000 Bulgarians and the forced migration of up to 561,844 Muslims.

Russian invasion of Manchuria 07.1900

The Russian invasion of Manchuria resulted in high casualties (at least in the thousands), with Russian forces killing many Manchus and burning their villages, causing thousands to flee.

Kishinev pogrom 19.04.1903

The Kishinev pogrom in 1903 resulted in 47 Jews killed, 92 severely wounded, and 500 injured, with over 1,500 homes and 600 stores damaged or pillaged.

Kyiv pogrom (1905) 18.10.1905

The 1905 Kyiv pogrom resulted in the massacre of approximately 100 Jews and serious injury to at least 300 more.

Odessa pogroms 18.10.1905

The Odesa pogroms, marked by anti-Jewish riots and attacks, caused over 500 Jewish casualties, 300 injuries, and widespread property destruction.

Białystok pogrom 14.06.1906

The Białystok pogrom, orchestrated by Russian authorities, resulted in the deaths of 88 people, including 82 Jews, and the plunder of 169 shops and houses.

Russian occupation of Tabriz 04.1909

The Russian occupation of Tabriz (1909-1918) lead to significant civilian casualties. 1,200 civilians and revolutionaries were executed during the occupation.

Central Asian revolt of 1916 07.1916

The Central Asian revolt of 1916, sparked by forced conscription into the Russian military, led to the death of 100,000 to 500,000 Central Asians due to violence, disease, and famine.
